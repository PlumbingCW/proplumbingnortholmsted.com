After the water stops, the repair depends on the pipe. Copper, PEX, galvanized steel and cast iron all fail differently, and the right fix depends on what is behind the wall.

When a burst pipe is an emergency

It is an emergency whenever water is escaping and you cannot stop it at a fixture. Do these steps in order.

  1. Close the main water valve. If you cannot find it, close the valve at the meter.
  2. Open a faucet at the lowest level to drain the line and relieve pressure.
  3. Turn off power to wet areas if you can reach the panel without standing in water.
  4. Move valuables and photograph the damage for insurance.
  5. Call the 24/7 line and tell us the pipe location if you know it.

Knowing where the main shutoff is before an emergency makes all the difference. In many homes it sits near the meter or where the water line enters the basement.

Frozen pipes: before and after they split

The Cleveland area sees January lows near 22 degrees on average, and cold snaps drop much lower. Pipes in exterior walls, garages and crawl spaces freeze first.

If a faucet runs slow, open it and apply gentle warmth from a hair dryer. Never use an open flame. If a frozen pipe has split, the leak starts when it thaws, so shut the main before thawing. Our crews repair the split section and check for other damage.

Cracked and broken pipe repair

Not every break is a flood. A hairline crack in a drain or supply line can leak slowly for months. A stain on a ceiling, a damp basement wall or a bill that creeps upward are signs.

Copper pipe repairs are common in 1960s homes. Pinhole leaks in copper often indicate wider corrosion, and we will tell you if more of the line is at risk. For finding a hidden leak first, see leak detection.

Drain pipe and cast iron repair

A cracked drain pipe sends wastewater into walls or under a floor. Cast iron stacks in older homes can crack at joints or rust through.

Repair can be a coupling, a replaced section or, in bad cases, a new stack. If the break is below the foundation or in the yard, it becomes a sewer job, covered under sewer line repair.

What to do about the damage

Stopping the water is step one. Drying the area quickly prevents mold, and that is a restoration job outside plumbing. We stop the leak, repair the pipe and give you notes that help with an insurance claim.

Document everything with photos. Keep the damaged pipe section if the plumber can save it. Standard homeowners policies often cover sudden water damage from a burst pipe but may not cover freezing in an unheated space, so read the terms.
