Who we are

This site is run by North Olmsted Pro Plumbing, 28895 Lorain Road, North Olmsted, OH 44070. You can reach us at contact@proplumbingnortholmsted.com or (440) 468-0237.

Phone calls

Our site is built around phone calls. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the person who answers.

We use the information you give on a call, such as your name, address and the problem, to dispatch a plumber and to follow up on the job.

This website

This site has no contact forms. Our hosting provider may log standard technical data such as IP address and browser type to keep the site working and secure. We do not sell your personal information.
