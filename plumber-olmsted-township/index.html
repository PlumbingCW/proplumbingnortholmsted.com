Olmsted Township is a civil township south of North Olmsted. It is not the same place as the City of Olmsted Falls, though they share a ZIP code and a school district.

If a pipe, drain or pump fails, call (440) 468-0237. The 24/7 line covers the township and dispatches from North Olmsted.

Who to call: permits, sewers and the township

Plumbing permits in the township go through the Cuyahoga County Board of Health, which lists Olmsted Township under its residential plumbing program. The township building department handles building and zoning permits, not plumbing.

Sewers are another split. The township says the county and the Northeast Ohio Regional Sewer District handle sewer and stormwater and that it has very limited authority over sewer repairs. A blockage in your own lateral is yours. A problem in the public sewer goes to the county.

Subdivisions, smoke tests and basement flooding

Township documents name subdivisions such as Glenbrook, Skyline, Greenbriar, Brentwood, Breezewood and the Villages of Lakeside. The county has smoke tested sanitary sewers in several of them, and a relining project in the Breezewood and Lakeside area was estimated at about $450,000.

A 2021 study found 17 areas needing stormwater action, and a collapsed culvert near Bagley and Old Fitch roads contributed to flooding. If your basement takes water, use the township's online basement flooding report form and call us for the plumbing side, such as a failing sump pump or a blocked lateral.

Homes from the 1980s and 1990s

Most township homes are younger than North Olmsted's. Their supply lines are copper or PEX and their drains are plastic. Common failures are builder-grade water heaters now near twenty years old, sump pumps and outdoor spigots.

Large lots mean long service lines and sometimes well or septic questions. Tell us if your property is on septic when you call. For buried line problems, see water line repair, and for a bill that jumps, try leak detection.

Roads, ZIP codes and where we work

Calls come from Bagley, Fitch, Schady, Usher and Cook roads and the streets between them. The township uses 44138, the same ZIP as Olmsted Falls, so tell us your street so we send the crew to the right place. We also cover 44017 in Berea and 44142 in Brook Park.

North of the township sits North Olmsted, and to the west is North Ridgeville, both within easy reach.

Codes and permits in Olmsted Township

Plumbing in the township follows the Ohio Plumbing Code, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). Plumbing permits go through the Cuyahoga County Board of Health instead of a city building department.

Because the permit path differs from nearby cities, ask us how it works before you schedule larger jobs such as a water heater replacement or a sewer repair.
