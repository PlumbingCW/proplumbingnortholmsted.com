Tank failed in the middle of the night: what to do

  1. Close the cold water supply valve at the top of the heater.
  2. Turn the breaker off for an electric unit, or the gas control to off for a gas unit.
  3. Open a hot tap upstairs to relieve pressure if the tank is still draining.
  4. Move items off the floor and mop what you can to limit damage.
  5. Call the 24/7 line so a technician can plan the right replacement unit.

Do not try to drain the tank unless you know the steps. A large tank holds 40 to 50 gallons of hot water.

Sizing the new heater to your house

A 40 gallon tank suits many households of one to three people. A family of four with a soaking tub often needs 50 gallons or a high-recovery unit. The decision comes from how many hot water fixtures run at once.

Tell us how many bathrooms you have and what runs at the same time. If a bigger tank will not fit, a tankless unit or a high-efficiency model may.

What the replacement day looks like

The technician shuts off the water, drains and removes the old unit, sets the new one on a pan or drain, connects supply, gas or electric, vents it, and tests for leaks and safe operation.

New work usually includes an expansion tank where the plumbing is a closed system, a new relief valve and pipe, and code-compliant venting. A permit may be needed and the plumber handles the filing. North Olmsted lists plumbing code as the 2024 Ohio Plumbing Code, based on the 2021 International Plumbing Code.

Permits and who pulls them

The city Building Division issues plumbing permits, and its application says the permit holder arranges inspections. Contractors must be registered with the city before permits are issued.

Rules vary by job, so we ask the Building Division when there is doubt rather than guess. A permitted installation also helps when you sell the house.

Old basement units and local housing

Water heaters in 1960s homes were often installed with minimal clearance, old galvanized fittings and a shared flue. Replacing the unit is a chance to correct those.

If you also see rust on supply pipes at the heater, ask about repiping at the same time. For a planned job in a new location, our water heater installation page explains the options.
