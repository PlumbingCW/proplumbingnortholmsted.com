When a water heater problem is an emergency

Not every failure is urgent, so it helps to know which signs mean act now.

  • Water pooling around the base of the tank. Shut the cold supply and, for electric heaters, switch off the breaker.
  • Water coming from the temperature and pressure relief pipe continuously, which can signal overpressure.
  • Rumbling, popping or a smell of gas near a gas heater. Leave and call the gas utility first.
  • Rusty or brown hot water from only the hot taps, which can mean the tank is corroding inside.

No hot water alone is uncomfortable, not dangerous. It still ranks high on urgency in winter, and we treat it as a same-day call whenever the schedule allows.

Leaking tank or leaking fitting?

Many leak calls turn out to be a fitting, a valve or the relief valve, not the tank. Those are repairable in minutes. A tank that leaks from the shell or the bottom is finished, because the steel liner has failed.

The technician will trace the water to its source before suggesting anything. If it is the tank, the honest answer is water heater replacement, since patching a corroded shell does not last.

Gas, electric and hybrid heaters

Most basement water heaters in North Olmsted homes are gas or electric tanks. Newer homes and replacements sometimes use hybrid heat pump models, which pull heat from the surrounding air and need room and a condensate drain.

Each type has its own parts and safety checks. Gas units need venting and combustion air. Hybrids need clear airflow and a drain. We repair all three and say plainly when a unit is past saving. For a planned swap, see water heater installation.

Repair or replace: a simple rule of thumb

Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years, depending on water quality and maintenance. A repair on a unit under about eight years old is usually worth it. A major repair on a unit over about ten may not be.

Sediment shortens life, and hardness matters. Cleveland Water's 2025 report lists hardness of 105 to 130 ppm (average 117) for its system, which is moderately hard. Check which utility serves your street and test your tap, because hard water leaves scale that stresses the tank and elements.

What a water heater visit includes

The technician checks the shutoff valves, the relief valve and pipe, the venting for gas units and the electrical supply for electric ones. A leak is traced before any part is replaced. You hear the price before work begins.

If the unit is a gas model in a basement, the technician also checks that the vent connects properly and that there is no sign of backdrafting. Safety comes before restoring hot water.

What to do before we arrive when the heater fails

  1. If the tank is leaking, shut off the cold water inlet and turn off power or gas to the heater.
  2. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 from outside.
  3. Do not touch scalding water at the relief valve discharge pipe.
  4. Open a hot faucet to relieve pressure if the tank is hot and bulging.
  5. Photograph the label and the leak so we can prepare the right parts.
