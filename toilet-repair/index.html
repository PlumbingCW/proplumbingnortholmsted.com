When a toilet problem is an emergency

A toilet that overflows, leaks from the base or backs up after other fixtures run is more than a nuisance. Wastewater damages floors and ceilings below.

  • Water rising to the rim and not draining. Stop flushing and close the supply valve.
  • Water seeping around the base. The wax ring or flange may have failed.
  • Bubbling or gurgling when other fixtures drain. The main line may be partly blocked.
  • A supply line spraying or dripping. Close the shutoff and replace the line.

If more than one drain is affected, the issue is likely the main line, covered under drain cleaning.

A toilet that leaks at the base

A rocking toilet or water on the floor after a flush often means a failed wax ring or a cracked flange. The wax ring seals the toilet to the drain. The flange is the plate on the floor that holds the toilet in place.

Repair means pulling the toilet, replacing the ring, repairing or replacing the flange and setting the toilet again. A rotted subfloor is a bigger repair, and we tell you if we see signs. A new wax ring on a damaged flange will fail again.

Toilet installation and replacement

Replacing a toilet is a good time to check the flange height, the shutoff valve and the floor. We set the new unit level, seal it and test the flush.

Older homes may have a toilet with a different rough-in distance. Measure from the wall to the center of the flange bolts, and tell us the number. Tank bolts, wax rings and supply lines are part of a proper installation. For a bathroom remodel that adds fixtures, see plumbing installation.

Wax rings, bolts and hardware

The small parts matter. A tank that rocks wears the gasket. Corroded bolts snap. Plastic bolts crack. We replace hardware when it looks worn, because a seal that fails after a month costs more than a new set of bolts.

Overflow cleanup and when to call for drains

After an overflow, wipe the floor, ventilate and check the ceiling below. Water can reach drywall and light fixtures.

If the overflow came from a main line clog, the toilet repair alone will not stop it. We clear the line and test every fixture before leaving. Related repeat clogs are discussed on our hydro jetting page.
