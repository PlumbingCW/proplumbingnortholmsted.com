A newer city with creeks that rise

The National Weather Service reported significant, widespread flood damage in North Ridgeville on May 12, 2014. An estimated 300 homes and 1,000 businesses were affected, and Mill Creek flooded condos on Mill Creek Boulevard, where 15 people were rescued from upper floors.

For homeowners, the lesson is practical. Know whether your lot drains toward a creek, keep the sump pump working with a battery backup and move valuables up before a forecast storm. Our sump pump page lists the options.

Homes from 2000 on: what fails and when

A house from 2003 is now over twenty years old. PEX and copper supply lines are in good shape, but the first water heater is near the end, a sump pump is working through its second decade and shower valves and toilets are starting to wear.

The typical call is water heater replacement, a failed fill valve or a pump that quit during a storm. Newer houses may also have a larger footprint, which means long supply runs that lose pressure and a larger softener or filter question. See water softener and filtration.
